# Nykaa-Spec-Campaign-WhoTaughtYou
A speculative Nykaa social media campaign celebrating the people who shape our beauty journeys. Through a Hero Reel and Instagram carousel, #WhoTaughtYou transforms everyday beauty lessons into relatable stories, encouraging Gen Z to share, tag, and pass their favourite beauty tricks forward.
# Nykaa - #WhoTaughtYou

## Project Overview

**#WhoTaughtYou** is a speculative social media campaign concept developed for **Nykaa**, exploring the emotional side of beauty and the people who shape our beauty journeys.

Instead of focusing solely on products, the campaign celebrates the everyday beauty lessons passed down through relationships-whether from a mother, sister, cousin, best friend, or beauty creator.

## Campaign Idea

**“Who taught you your favourite beauty trick?”**

The campaign turns familiar beauty rituals into personal stories. It highlights how beauty knowledge is often shared informally and how these small moments can build confidence and create lasting memories.

## Target Audience

* Gen Z and young millennials
* Indian beauty and makeup enthusiasts
* Social-media-active consumers
* Consumers interested in relatable, authentic beauty content

## Campaign Objectives

* Build an emotional connection with Nykaa's audience
* Encourage user-generated content and participation
* Position Nykaa as part of consumers' personal beauty journeys
* Create highly shareable and relatable Instagram content

## Content Developed

### 01. Hero Reel - “Who Taught You to Wing Your Eyeliner?”

A cinematic four-frame story following a young woman's journey from learning eyeliner from her elder sister to eventually teaching the same beauty trick to someone else.

**CTA:** “Tag them. Pass it on.”
**Hashtag:** #WhoTaughtYou

### 02. Instagram Carousel - “Five Beauty Lessons We Never Forget”

A six-slide editorial carousel featuring five memorable beauty lessons, including skincare, mascara, blush, and foundation techniques. Each lesson is connected to the person who likely taught it.

The final slide encourages viewers to reflect on their own beauty mentors and tag them.

## Visual Direction

The campaign follows a premium, editorial beauty aesthetic inspired by Nykaa's social presence.

**Key visual cues:**

* Blush pink, white, beige, and warm neutral tones
* Minimalist layouts
* Premium editorial photography
* Realistic Indian settings and women
* Natural emotions and expressions
* Elegant typography
* Soft cinematic lighting
* Authentic skin texture
* Subtle luxury rather than excessive glamour

## Creative Approach

The campaign combines **storytelling + nostalgia + beauty education + social participation**. Rather than presenting beauty as a perfect or aspirational standard, it presents it as something learned, shared, and passed forward.

The use of AI-generated visual concepts helped explore realistic campaign compositions while maintaining a premium commercial photography direction.

## Key Message

**Beauty is not just what we learn. It's who we learn it from.**

The campaign aims to make Nykaa feel less like a beauty retailer and more like a **companion in the consumer's beauty journey**, encouraging audiences to share personal memories and celebrate the people who helped them become confident in their own beauty routines.
